# My event preferences

Agents read this file before every sweep and use it to decide what makes the
calendar. Edit it whenever your tastes change — no other configuration needed.

## Who this calendar is for

A curious 31-year-old straight man who has just moved to San Francisco and
lives in SoMa (39 Tehama St, near the Salesforce Transit Center). He likes
learning new things, dancing, being social, and discovering the city — not a
tourist checklist. New in town, so events where it's easy to meet people
matter more than ever.

## Things I'm interested in

- Dance, high priority: ecstatic dance, 5Rhythms (Bay Area events only),
  swing / lindy hop and West Coast Swing socials, conscious movement — classes
  and socials alike
- Contra dance and English country dance (the US cousin of the ceilidhs I
  loved in London) — Bay Area Country Dance Society and similar
- Relational and embodiment practices, high priority: circling, authentic
  relating games/workshops, Alexander Technique lessons and intro workshops,
  contact improvisation (jams, classes, beginner-friendly labs)
- Rationality community: LessWrong / ACX / EA / adjacent meetups and talks —
  the Bay Area is the community's home (Mox in the Mission, Lighthaven in
  Berkeley)
- Dating and singles events: quality singles socials (hobby-based or
  activity-based ones especially). Only mixed male/female events whose age
  bracket includes 31 (e.g. 23-35, 28-40); skip LGBTQ+-specific, women-only,
  and 40+ events
- Hands-on classes and workshops: pottery/ceramics, cooking, crafts, life drawing
- Live music, especially indie, folk, and small intimate venues — small rooms
  in SoMa and the Mission are a top priority (think Hotel Utah, Bottom of the
  Hill, Make-Out Room, Café du Nord: 50-300 capacity)
- Folk and trad: folk clubs, Irish trad sessions, acoustic nights
- Science and technology talks, lectures, interesting panel discussions —
  including AI safety events (Mox and the AI Safety San Francisco calendar are
  favourite sources), Long Now, Exploratorium After Dark, Cal Academy NightLife
- Food events: supper clubs, food markets, tastings
- Outdoor stuff: SF's mild weather means park events, festivals and outdoor
  cinema are worth including year-round (Golden Gate Park, Dolores Park,
  Presidio, Fort Mason)
- Art exhibitions worth a dedicated visit
- Social/community events where it's easy to meet people

## Location

- Home base is **SoMa (39 Tehama St)**. Anything within ~25 minutes by bike
  gets priority: SoMa, Mission, Hayes Valley, Civic Center/Tenderloin,
  Financial District, North Beach, Dogpatch, Potrero Hill, Castro
- The rest of San Francisco is fine
- East Bay (Oakland, Berkeley) only if the event is really special — it's a
  ~20–30 min BART ride from Montgomery/Embarcadero
- Peninsula / South Bay / Marin: skip unless truly exceptional

## Things to skip

- Generic bar crawls, club nights (dance events are the exception)
- Corporate networking events and startup pitch nights
- Anything aimed mainly at tourists
- Kids/family-only events
- Events costing more than ~$80 unless truly exceptional

## Practical constraints

- Weekday events should start at 6pm or later; weekends any time
- BREADTH over strict curation: I want plenty of options each day. Aim for
  8–15 new events per sweep. Include "decent fits", not only great ones —
  when in doubt, add it. The hard skips (club nights, tourist traps,
  mismatched dating demographics, outside the Bay Area) still apply, but an
  event only needs to be plausibly appealing, not exceptional
